ALWAYS (Brand Rules)

  • Use BEAD5 as one unified campaign name.
  • Use "Five Moments. One Journey." as the primary tagline.
  • Identify Jesus Youth — Pazhassiraja College as the organizer.
  • Integrate the number 5 naturally into the wordmark.
  • Maintain clean, modern, youthful, and minimal aesthetics.

NEVER (Brand Rules)

  • Never separate BEAD and 5 into two independent symbols.
  • Never replace BEAD5 with "Bead Five".
  • Never use neon or aggressive decorative religious fonts.
  • Never make the design look like a generic church program announcement.
  • Never display the technical domain BEAD5JYPRC as the primary logo.
